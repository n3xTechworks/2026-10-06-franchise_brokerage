Buyer's guide

Be Ready Before You Make an Offer

Serious sellers favour prepared buyers. Having these items in order helps you move quickly and negotiate from a position of strength.

  • Proof of funds – a bank or lender letter showing your deposit and financing capacity.
  • Signed NDA – unlocks confidential financials and the seller's identity.
  • Personal financial statement – lenders and sellers both ask for it early.
  • Due diligence plan – a team of accountant, attorney and inspector lined up in advance.
  • Clear criteria – target industry, budget, location and how involved you want to be.
