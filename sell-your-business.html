Business valuation

Know What Your Business Is Worth

A realistic price attracts serious buyers and prevents deals from collapsing in due diligence. We combine three proven methods into one defensible valuation.

Earnings multiples

We apply industry-specific multiples to your normalised earnings (SDE or EBITDA), based on recent comparable sales in your sector.

Asset & market comparison

Equipment, inventory, lease terms and goodwill are tested against live listings and closed transactions in your market.

Owner-benefit normalisation

We adjust for your salary, one-off expenses and personal costs so buyers see the true cash flow the business generates.

Seller FAQ

Questions Sellers Ask Us

Straight answers to the questions we hear most before owners decide to sell.

How long does it take to sell a business?

Most of our sales close in three to five months. Timing depends on your industry, price, the quality of your records and how quickly a buyer's financing is approved. Well-prepared businesses sell fastest.

Will my employees and customers find out?

No. We market your business anonymously and only reveal its name after a buyer has signed an NDA and passed our financial screening. Staff, suppliers and competitors are not told until you choose.

What does it cost to list with you?

The valuation is free. After that we work on a success-based commission, paid at closing, so our incentives are aligned with yours. We explain the fee structure in writing before you sign anything.

What documents will I need to provide?

Typically three years of tax returns and profit and loss statements, a current balance sheet, your lease, an equipment list and an overview of staff and operations. We help you assemble and organise everything.

Can I keep working in the business during the sale?

Yes, and you should. We handle buyer enquiries, meetings and negotiations so day-to-day performance stays strong. Consistent results are the best way to protect your price.
