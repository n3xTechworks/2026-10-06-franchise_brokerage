FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often before a first consultation.

What does the first consultation involve?

It is a free 45-minute conversation, in person or by video. We listen to your goals, outline realistic options and explain the process and timing. There is no obligation to continue.

How do you keep my sale confidential?

Listings are anonymous and describe the business without naming it. Every interested buyer is screened for financial capability and must sign a non-disclosure agreement before receiving your company name or financials.

How much do your services cost?

Buyers and franchise seekers are not charged for our guidance. On the sell side we work on a success-based commission agreed in writing before we start, so you only pay when your business sells.

How long does it take to sell a business?

Most sales close in six to nine months from the day we list. Well-prepared financials, a realistic asking price and flexible buyers can shorten that, while complex or seasonal businesses may take longer.

Can you help me buy my first business?

Yes. We help you define your budget and criteria, shortlist suitable businesses, review financials, introduce lenders and coordinate due diligence through to closing.

Do you work with franchise buyers as well as independent businesses?

We do. Our franchise consultants compare brands, review disclosure documents and fees, assess territories and support you through opening, whether it is your first unit or your fifth.
