About Us

A Chicago advisory team that helps owners sell with confidence and helps buyers and franchisees invest with their eyes open.

Our story

Built by dealmakers who have sat on both sides of the table

Franchise Consulting & Business Brokerage began in 2010 when two Chicago advisors grew tired of watching good businesses sell for too little – and eager buyers overpay for companies they never fully understood. We set out to build a firm that put clear numbers, honest advice and patient guidance ahead of the quick close.

Today we are a team of brokers, valuation analysts and franchise specialists. Whether you are retiring from a business you built over decades, buying your first independent shop, or opening a franchise unit, we manage the process from first conversation to closing day.

  • Confidential buyer screening on every sell-side engagement
  • Independent valuations built on real financials, not guesswork
  • One dedicated advisor from introduction to closing

Our values

The principles behind every engagement

Buying or selling a business is personal. These four commitments guide how we work with every client.

1

Integrity

We give you the honest answer, even when it is not the one you hoped for. Our fees are agreed up front and never tied to pushing a deal through.

2

Confidentiality

Employees, customers and competitors should not learn about a sale before you are ready. Every buyer signs an NDA before seeing any detail.

3

Transparency

You see the same numbers we do: valuation methods, buyer feedback and offer terms, explained in plain language at every step.

4

Results

We measure ourselves by closed deals and successful openings, not by the number of listings. Your outcome is the only metric that counts.
